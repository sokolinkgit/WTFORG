Our story

Three stages.
One clear lineage.

HWDI's history is intentionally documented — because the origin of an idea, the formation of an initiative and the registration of an institution are not the same event.

Rooted in Eastlands, Nairobi. Built for Africa.

  1. 2018 The root

    The idea takes root

    Donnel Israel begins Mtaani Youth Network (MYN) in Eastlands, Nairobi. Young people are not only beneficiaries of development — they become creators of solutions, opportunities and transformation.

    Origin of the vision. Not a legal registration date.

  2. 2024–2026 The initiative

    A philosophy becomes a platform

    The Healing With Donnel Israel Initiative takes organised form. A four-pillar social-impact framework expands with Spiritual Growth to become today's five-pillar model.

    21 August 2026 — institutional recognition date.

  3. 21.08.2026 The institution

    Two arms, one ecosystem

    The founding framework formally separates the social-impact Initiative from the emerging commercial HWDI Group of Companies — enterprise in service of mission.

    Institutional structuring — not incorporation.

What comes next

The ecosystem. Companies, brands, programmes, movements and partnerships connect into an institution capable of operating across Kenya and contributing to Africa's transformation — founder-led, then team-led, institution-led, ecosystem-led.

See the ecosystem

Origin and registration are recorded separately. The 2018 historical origin date is not a legal registration date. The company registration date will be inserted into the institutional record once the official certificate is issued.

The HWDI theory of transformation

A pathway,
not a programme.

Sustainable transformation is not an event. It is a journey that begins with healing and ends with people who can transform their own communities.

  1. 01

    Heal

    Address wounds, mental health and wellbeing.

  2. 02

    Discover

    Help people discover identity, purpose and potential.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Build the skills, character and support systems that turn potential into capacity.

  4. 04

    Create

    Turn ideas, talents and skills into opportunities and enterprises.

  5. 05

    Connect

    Connect people to markets, mentors, partners, jobs and resources.

  6. 06

    Transform

    Enable individuals and organisations to create measurable community impact.

✦ The goal is people who create solutions, companies that create opportunities, and movements that create change.

The enterprise ecosystem

One mission.
Three engines.

The social mission must not be lost in the pursuit of commercial growth. The commercial arm must be strong enough to fund the mission — creating resources, employment and opportunity.

A · Owned 60–100%

HWDI-owned brands

Businesses, products, platforms and companies where HWDI holds significant or controlling ownership — built from our intellectual property, programmes and entrepreneurial opportunities.

  • Built and scaled directly
  • Mission-aligned ownership
  • Revenue that funds impact
B · Incubated 10–30%

HWDI-incubated brands

Businesses created with founders and strategic partners, owned jointly — supported with the resources, networks and systems that help them grow.

  • Business development & incubation
  • Branding, media & technology
  • Training, mentorship & market access
C · Partner Shared value

HWDI partner brands

Independent organisations that collaborate with HWDI on programmes, events, training, sponsorship, media, technology, employment, investment or research.

  • Strategic collaboration
